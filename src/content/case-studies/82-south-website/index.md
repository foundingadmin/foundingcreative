With a new brand in hand, 82 South needed a website that sells the feeling of their events and stays fresh after every show.

## The challenge

- A brand-new identity had no home online.
- Five service lines needed one clear path to a quote.
- New event work had to go live fast, with no developer on staff.

### Our approach

- Design straight from the Brand Blueprint, using its headlines, squircles and color.
- Make the portfolio the engine: filterable and easy to post to.
- Hand over the keys with training, analytics and a care plan.

#### The process

## Blueprint to browser

Built alongside the brand, launched the same month.

1. **Sitemap** built around how buyers shop: Services, About, Portfolio and Contact.
2. **Wireframes** for the homepage, services, portfolio and project templates.
3. **UI design** with brand headlines, wave patterns and squircle-framed photography.
4. **Homepage hero** that cycles Events, Exhibits and Activations above the positioning line.
5. **Services page** covering five offerings and the Discover, Design, Deliver process.
6. **Filterable portfolio** sorted by Event, Exhibit, Media, Talent and Activation.
7. **Project template** for each event, with location, scope and a photo gallery.
8. **About page** with founder story, leadership, awards and a global footprint map: 45 cities, 10 countries.
9. **WordPress build** on the Pro theme and Cornerstone, kept lean with The SEO Framework and GA4.
10. **Launch on Sep 16, 2024**, the same day the client asked to go live.
11. **Training** on image and copy edits and on posting new portfolio projects.
12. [**Site care plan**](/site-care-plan/) for updates, fixes and new page templates after launch.
