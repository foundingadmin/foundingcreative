With a new brand in hand, 82 South needed a website that sells the feeling of their events and stays fresh after every show.

## The challenge

- A brand-new identity had no home online.
- Five service lines needed one clear path to a quote.
- New event work had to go live fast, with no developer on staff.

### Our approach

- Design straight from the Brand Blueprint, using its headlines, squircles and color.
- Make the portfolio the engine: filterable and easy to post to.
- Hand over the keys with training, analytics and a care plan.
