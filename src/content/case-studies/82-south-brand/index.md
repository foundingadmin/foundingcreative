After 15 years of producing events for national brands, 82 South needed an identity as memorable as the experiences it builds.

## The challenge

- 82 South's work had outgrown its brand.
- Its services spanned events, exhibits, media, activations and logistics, with no single story tying them together.
- The team needed brand tools it could use every day, without an agency in the loop.

### Our approach

- Define the strategy before touching a logo.
- Build one flexible system: a wordmark, a badge, a color palette and patterns.
- Package everything into a single Brand Blueprint, with download-ready toolkits linked on every page.

#### The process

## From strategy to swag

One system, built in the order a brand gets used.

1. **Discovery** with founders Brian and Matt Riha and Managing Director DeAnna Miller.
2. **Strategic framework**, including the mission "To stage your vision into every lasting moment."
3. **Positioning**: sensory experiences that go deeper than the event itself.
4. **Six marketing pillars**, ranked to guide content from the top of the funnel to the bottom.
5. **Personality and tone**: Insightful. Adaptive. Charismatic. Paired with clear do and don't examples.
6. **Headline library** built on each pillar, ready for ads, decks and social posts.
7. **Typography** set in Mulish, from Regular to Black, for a clear hierarchy.
8. **Color system** led by 82 South purple and checked against AAA accessibility (7:1 contrast).
9. **Logo system**: horizontal and vertical wordmarks, plus a badge whose arrow always points south.
10. **Patterns and squircle shapes** that brand any surface in seconds.
11. **Corporate identity**: HTML email signatures, business cards, Word letterhead, PowerPoint and one sheet templates.
12. **Brand activations**: apparel, hats, luggage, tumblers, packaging, office signage and a stenciled road case.

## A North Star that points South

### Mission, vision, positioning

![82 South Brand Blueprint strategic framework page with the mission, vision, positioning and personality](./strategic-framework.webp)

We wrote three lines the team could repeat anywhere. The vision, "To be the South Star in leading a better kind of partnership," gave the badge its meaning.

### Six pillars, one funnel

![82 South marketing pillar hierarchy, a funnel from sensory enhancing at the top to growth, client care and expertise at the bottom](./pillar-hierarchy.webp)

Sensory enhancing leads at the top. Innovative partnership and bespoke experiences sit in the middle. Growth, client care and expertise close the deal at the bottom.

## An identity with a sense of direction

### The badge

![82 South logo system: horizontal wordmark, vertical wordmark and the badge](./logo-system.webp)

![82 South badge in its approved purple, grey, black and white color versions](./badge.webp)

The 82 sits inside a squircle, and a lime arrow below it points south. Rotating the badge is off limits: the arrow has to point the right way.

### Color built for contrast

![82 South color palette swatches: purple, light purple, lime green, white, black and grey](./color-palette.webp)

The palette is purple, light purple, lime green, grey, black and white. Every approved pairing passes AAA contrast, so the brand stays readable on stage screens and in print.

### Mulish everywhere

![82 South typography page showing Mulish in Regular, Medium, Bold and Black weights](./mulish-weights.webp)

One open-source typeface works across the web, Office and Google, so nobody hunts for a font file.

### Conclusion

<a href="https://82south.com" rel="noopener">82 South</a> had built a reputation for flawless events, but its brand didn't show it. We gave the team a <a href="/brandforge/">strategy<span class="visually-hidden"> (BrandForge, our brand strategy service)</span></a> they could say in one breath, and a visual system that flexes from a business card to a trade show booth.

The Brand Blueprint put every tool in one place. Two years later, the 82 South team is still running the brand themselves, from LinkedIn posts to event signage, and it still looks like one company.

### Key outcomes

#### A positioning they own

"Sensory experiences that go deeper than the event itself" now headlines the 82 South homepage.

#### A self-serve brand

Templates for email, decks, letterhead and sales sheets let the team ship on-brand work without a designer.

#### Brand to website, one system

The blueprint became the foundation for the new 82south.com, which launched the same month.
