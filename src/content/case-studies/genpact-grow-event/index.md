Taking on the mission to reimagine Genpact's Grow, we set out to do more than just execute an event. We aimed to elevate it into a fresh new experience that sticks.

## The challenge

Genpact needed a standout digital experience to elevate their flagship event and engage a global audience.

- No unified digital hub for event content or speakers
- Visuals didn’t reflect the event’s scale or energy
- Limited ways to create excitement before and after the event

### Our approach

We brought the Grow Event to life with a bold, interactive experience that matched its ambition.

- Designed a dynamic, branded microsite to anchor the event
- Showcased speakers, sessions, and content with impact
- Created a system to scale for future events

![Genpact meeting event mockup of large screens](./image-3.webp)

user journey mapping

## Event creative done right

1. Conduct analysis of Genpact’s brand, previous events, and audience demographics.
2. Establish a visual language that is threaded through the event, from digital to on-site.
3. Travel to London to iterate and collaborate on art direction at Genpact headquarters.
4. Rolled out visual identity across event materials, ensuring each piece communicated effectively.
5. Oversaw the production of all design elements, ensuring quality and consistency.
6. Managed art and design aspects during the event, ensuring an immersive experience.

<p class="statement">Securing success with Grow was not just a goal. It was imperative to redefining foundations towards a new standard for their future events to engage and inspire.</p>

To amplify attendee engagement, we engineered interactive game-style solutions turning learning into an adventurous quest. This gamified experience delved into Genpact's latest technical solutions, transforming complex information into a fun and captivating journey, thereby enhancing the educational aspect with a touch of play.

### Sky-high innovation

Our custom-built Drone Scanner Arcade cabinet took center stage, encapsulating Genpact's cutting-edge remote auditing solutions for insurance claims in an immersive, interactive kiosk game. By gamifying the experience, we translated Genpact's technical prowess into an engaging narrative that allowed attendees to virtually pilot drones, showcasing the future of commercial insurance assessments.

## A vision realized

### Conclusion

As the curtains fell on Grow18, the success of the event was etched not just in the memories of those who attended, but in the tangible outcomes that followed. Attendees left engaged, through interactive installations and digital interfaces registering high participation levels, indicating a deeper connection with the content and brand. Secondly, post-event feedback highlighted an unprecedented appreciation for the immersive brand experience, leading to an enhanced perception of Genpact as not just a corporate entity, but as a beacon of innovation and transformation in the industry. These outcomes not only reflect the fruition of our creative endeavors but also set a new benchmark for Genpact's future corporate events.

### Key outcomes

#### Increased engagement

Through interactive experiences, conversations were sparked, connections were deeper, and otherwise passive viewing turned into active experiencing.

#### Quality perception

The unified visual identity at Grow enhanced Genpact's brand perception, embodying excellence and innovation at every event touchpoint.
